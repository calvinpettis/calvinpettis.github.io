Introductory Post (8/20/2025)

My best in-class computing experience would probably be when coding started to click for me around CS1 or two. I did not know computers more than basic GUI operation when I first transferred here my junior year (I was a biology major at the time), and felt a little overwhelmed and confused in class. There was a constant feeling that I was always missing some important information that was supposed to be a given. I think it is when I saw a function that was a "boolean foo()" used as an input to a boolean variable, that the whole picture started fitting together more clearly and methods, returns, and functions started to make sense. Just like with any skill I have practiced, like exercise and learning a new instrument, results take time and practice and it is good to remember that consistency beats talent almost any day of the week.

The best out-of-class computing experience I have had was probably working on the data structures assignments. I would hit "run" on a solution of mine, and come back an hour later to see that it still had not solved the problem. When you got the solution to run in 0.1 seconds compared to 1hr+, it is very exciting to see the results of the hard work and correct algorithms at play. That class gave me a sense of how a project could handle scaling up if needed and has helped me optimize other assignments.
